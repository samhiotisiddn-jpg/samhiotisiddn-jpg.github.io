Why this matters

When someone searches “dinner Albury” or “wedding venue Wodonga”, the businesses below are who they find. But a well-known name doesn’t mean a healthy website — and an unhealthy website quietly loses bookings every week: pages that load slowly on a phone get abandoned, missing descriptions mean Google guesses what you do, and broken mobile layouts send customers to the next result.

Every issue listed here is fixable, most in under a day of work. I’ve graded each site on the percentage of 23 checks it passed.

The results

The River Deck 78% — C+

riverdeckcafe.com.au · cafe & restaurant, Noreuil Park
  • Takes 3.5 seconds before the page even starts loading (TTFB 3523ms) — most mobile visitors leave before 3s.
  • No online booking or enquiry form anywhere on the site — customers have to phone during business hours.
  • Homepage is 697KB of HTML — about 7× heavier than it needs to be.
  • 39 of 41 images have no alt text — invisible to Google Images and screen readers.
  • Page title is 110 characters — Google cuts it off mid-sentence in search results.

Albury Entertainment Centre 70% — C

alburyentertainmentcentre.com.au · council-run events venue
  • No meta description at all — Google writes its own (usually bad) summary of the venue in search results.
  • Zero H1 headings on the homepage — search engines can’t tell what the page is about.
  • 54 of 57 images missing alt text.
  • No Open Graph tags — shared links on Facebook show a blank, imageless preview.
  • No enquiry form; slow server response (2.5s TTFB); no sitemap.

MAMA — Murray Art Museum Albury 83% — B−

mamalbury.com.au · art museum
  • Homepage HTML weighs 1,049KB — the heaviest of the five, heavy enough to stutter on older phones.
  • Only 1 of 6 standard security headers present.
  • No H1 heading; no sitemap to help Google index exhibitions.

Drop Bear Coffee 87% — B+

dropbearcoffee.com.au · café & roastery
  • Best of the five — but the server still takes 4.4 seconds to respond (slowest of the group).
  • Page title doesn’t mention Albury — a free local-SEO win going unused.
  • 7 of 19 images missing alt text.

SS&A Club 0% — site gone

ssanda.com.au · club & venue
  • The domain no longer resolves to a website at all — it currently shows a domain-parking page. Anyone searching for the club online right now finds a dead end. If this is your business, this is a five-alarm fix.

The pattern

Not one of the five passed everything. The same three problems appeared almost everywhere: slow servers (4 of 5 took over 2 seconds to respond), missing alt text (over 100 undescribed images across the group), and missing or bloated page structure. None of these require a redesign — they’re one-day fixes that directly affect whether a customer books or bounces.
