Trust

✓ Served over HTTPS
✗ 1/6 security headers P1
Why it matters: Missing headers let attackers hijack forms, inject content, or trick your customers. Fixable in one config block.
Fix: Add Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, and a Referrer-Policy at the server.
✗ No HSTS P2
Why it matters: Without HSTS, a customer on café Wi-Fi can be silently downgraded to HTTP.
Fix: Add Strict-Transport-Security once HTTPS is confirmed stable.

Speed

✗ First byte in 3118ms P1
Why it matters: Slow first byte = hosting or server-side bottleneck. Phones on patchy reception give up around 3s.
Fix: Enable server caching, upgrade cheap shared hosting, or move to a CDN in front.
✗ 2998KB HTML P2
Why it matters: Very heavy pages load slowly on mobile data and cost impatient visitors.
Fix: Compress images, lazy-load below-the-fold media, remove unused plugins/scripts.
